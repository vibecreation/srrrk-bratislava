OUR STORY

Younger sibling of a Bratislava favorite

Tucked into the courtyard of the Primate's Palace, SRRRK is part lemonade bar, part café, part wine and beer bar, part reading room — the younger sibling of Bratislava's beloved Baryk, from the same team behind branding studio Echt and small-batch soda maker @majami.drinks. No hot food, no hard liquor, just a genuinely easy place to feel good in one of the city's most beautiful courtyards.
